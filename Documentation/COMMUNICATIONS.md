# Communications Specialist

**Status:** Canonical Team role; Discord plan approved, not implemented
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-06

The Communications specialist is the unseen Team member responsible for external human-message channels. Modesty remains the only conversational presence and reports what the specialist has found or prepared. Communications may carry a Researcher's finished result or a Fishing Buddy's advice, but does not perform their research or interpretation.

## Platform and identity boundary

Discord is the first platform. Every connection is restricted to Drew's explicitly authorized account context, server, channel and operation. Credentials and tokens use private operating-system-protected storage and must never be written to chat, source control, the Filing Cabinet, or the Bookshelf. Modesty must visibly distinguish disconnected, connected, reading, drafting, waiting for approval, sending, and failed states.

The specialist may not impersonate Drew, conceal automation where platform or community rules require disclosure, bypass access controls, scrape inaccessible history, monitor unrelated channels, retain messages indefinitely, or silently contact anyone. Removing authorization must stop future access cleanly.

## First Discord duty

The smallest useful, safe duty is:

1. connect using the reviewed Discord integration route;
2. access one server and one channel chosen by Drew;
3. retrieve only a bounded recent-message window;
4. identify direct mentions, unanswered questions and plausibly actionable items without pretending uncertain intent is fact;
5. return a concise summary through Modesty with message authorship and time context;
6. prepare a reply draft when requested;
7. display the exact destination and exact draft for approval;
8. send only after Drew explicitly approves that unchanged draft;
9. record a content-minimal audit of access, approval, outcome and failure;
10. fail closed on permission, identity, destination, draft, network or rate-limit changes.

Reading does not authorize drafting. Drafting does not authorize sending. Approval applies to one exact draft and destination and expires when either changes.

## Dependencies and later growth

The first implementation follows Modesty's local Voice foundation and the minimum background-presence host, because Discord must remain usable while the visual Study is hidden. Text-only Discord comes before voice-channel participation.

Later reviewed increments may add selected-channel notification rules, scheduled summaries, attachments with media quarantine, multi-channel triage, remote-device presentation, and narrowly scoped autonomous replies. Discord voice channels require the same explicit microphone and speaker controls as local Voice plus separate channel-entry, recording, disclosure, interruption and exit rules.

The specialist's moniker, office device, outfit and Bobblehead remain deliberately undecided. They do not block the functional contract.
